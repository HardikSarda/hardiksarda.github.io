Flagship project · Aug 2025 – Sept 2026

Formula Student car PR26

PR26 is a KTM RC 390-powered combustion Formula Student car on an AISI 4130 space frame, built over one season for SUPRA SAEINDIA 2026 at the Buddh International Circuit. The case study covers the frame and upright FEA, the brake, aero and intake work, and how the car was assembled and documented.
